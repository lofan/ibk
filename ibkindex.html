The Psychology of Money

Morgan Housel

Featured Book Breakdown • 8 Min Read

Doing well with money has a little to do with how smart you are and a lot to do with how you behave.

Financial success is not a hard science. It’s a soft skill, where how you behave is more important than what you know.

  • Freedom is the ultimate dividend: The highest form of wealth is the ability to wake up every morning and say, "I can do whatever I want today."
  • Getting rich vs. Staying rich: Getting rich requires taking risks and optimism. Staying rich requires humility and paranoia.

Essential Mental Models

Inversion (Charlie Munger)

"All I want to know is where I'm going to die, so I'll never go there."

Instead of thinking about how to achieve success, focus on how to avoid failure. Solve problems backwards.

Margin of Safety (Benjamin Graham)

"Build a bridge that can hold 30,000 pounds when you only drive 10,000-pound trucks."

Always leave a cushion for error, unpredictable shocks, and human miscalculation in financial and life decisions.
